# Source quality policy (planned)

No automated quality score ships in the current prototype. This is the intended evaluation rubric.

---

## Criteria (future)

1. **Authority** — official company, regulator, or reputable business press vs anonymous blogs.
2. **Date** — published-at present and recent enough for the claim type.
3. **Independence** — third-party reporting vs the subject’s own marketing page.
4. **Specificity** — named city, headcount, or date vs vague “plans to expand”.
5. **Corroboration** — second source agrees.
6. **Integrity** — content hash unchanged since retrieval.

## Limitations

- Scores are opinions encoded as policy, not ground truth.
- Paywalled or PDFs may be incomplete.
- AI extraction can misread tables; human review remains mandatory for high-impact claims.
- This policy does not authorize scraping that violates source terms.

## Product rules

- Never hide a low-quality source if it is the only support for a shown claim; label it.
- Do not average incompatible numbers to “improve” a score.
