# Personas

These personas are **hypotheses**. They have not been validated with interviews in this repository. Do not treat them as committed ICP until research says so.

---

## GCC expansion / location team

- Chooses or expands an India GCC site.
- Needs city and talent comparisons with citations, not marketing scores.
- Will distrust a number that cannot be opened to a source.

## Research analyst / reviewer

- Ingests articles, filings, and company pages.
- Uses AI extraction as a draft, then approves or rejects claims.
- Needs a queue, clear claim text, and evidence quotes.

## Ecosystem service provider

- Sells real estate, talent, legal, or facilities services into GCCs.
- Wants accurate maps of who is where—without scraping Pulse for unverified rumors.

## Economic-development / policy user

- Tracks announcements, jobs, and cluster growth.
- Needs dated signals and conservative language around unconfirmed items.

---

Existing UI already sketches companies, cities, providers, signals, extract, and review. That does **not** mean each persona’s workflow is complete.
